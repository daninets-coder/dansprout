# Story Sprout Marketing Brief

## Product In One Sentence

Story Sprout helps parents, guardians, and teachers create deeply personalized reading stories with children, using the child's name, interests, reading level, learning goal, chosen story world, and adventure idea to make reading practice more engaging.

## What The Website Offers

Story Sprout is an adult-managed reading experience for children. An adult creates a learner profile, chooses a grade level and reading skill, and enters an adventure idea. The child can help shape the story by choosing interests, themes, characters, and possibilities. Story Sprout then creates a personalized story that feels made for that reader and that the adult and child can read, discuss, and explore together.

The product combines:

- Personalized AI-generated stories
- Grade-aware reading practice
- Curriculum-linked reading objectives
- Multiple-choice comprehension checks
- Automatic story-check scoring
- Vocabulary support
- Read-aloud tools
- Saved stories and learner progress
- Adult privacy and data controls

## Personalization

Each story can be shaped around the individual reader. Personalization may include:

- The learner's first name
- Age range and selected grade level
- Favorite interests, such as animals, space, sports, music, or cooking
- Topics the family wants to avoid
- A reading skill or curriculum objective
- A story world, such as a garden, ocean, castle, or custom world
- The adventure the child wants to explore
- A selected story length: Quick read, Standard story, or Longer adventure

The goal is for the child to recognize their own interests in the story while still practicing a purposeful reading skill.

## Planned Printed Story Delivery

Story Sprout also has a planned future feature for families who want a physical keepsake. With the parent's request and approval, a generated story could be prepared for printing and mailed to the parent.

Possible future printed-story features include:

- A formatted storybook version of a saved digital story
- Printed pages and cover artwork
- Optional personalization with the learner's name and story details
- Parent-controlled ordering and shipping information
- A mailed keepsake for bedtime reading, gifting, or a child's bookshelf

Printed story delivery is planned and should be marketed as **coming later**, not as an available feature until printing, payment, shipping, fulfillment, privacy, and customer-support workflows are implemented and tested.

## Who It Is For

### Primary Audience

Parents and guardians who want a more personal and enjoyable way to support a child's reading practice at home.

### Secondary Audience

Teachers and authorized educators who want flexible story-based reading practice for individual learners or small classroom groups.

### The Child's Role

The adult manages the account and learner profile. The child can help choose interests, story worlds, characters, and adventures. The child reads or listens to the story and answers questions based on what they read.

Children do not create their own accounts.

## The Core Experience

1. An adult creates an account.
2. The adult adds a learner and selects an age range.
3. The adult chooses a grade level and reading skill.
4. The adult and child choose a story idea, world, or adventure.
5. Story Sprout creates a personalized reading story.
6. The child reads or listens to the story.
7. The child answers multiple-choice comprehension questions.
8. Story Sprout provides an automatic story-check score.
9. The adult and child can retry, discuss the story, review vocabulary, and continue practicing.

## Why Families May Use It

### Makes Reading Personal

The story can reflect the child's interests, such as animals, space, sports, cooking, fantasy, nature, or an adventure the child imagines.

### Gives Reading Practice A Purpose

Each story is connected to a selected reading goal and grade-level curriculum objective. The parent can see the skill being practiced in plain language.

### Encourages Conversation

The story creates an opportunity for an adult and child to read together, discuss ideas, explain answers, and talk about new words.

### Supports Confidence

The experience is designed to make reading feel approachable and meaningful rather than like an isolated worksheet or test.

### Adapts To Different Readers

The selected grade level guides sentence length, vocabulary, story structure, comprehension questions, and expected complexity.

### Offers Flexible Story Lengths

Families can choose:

- **Quick read:** approximately 100-180 words
- **Standard story:** approximately 180-350 words
- **Longer adventure:** approximately 350-700 words

The app uses the selected grade level together with the length choice to guide story generation.

## Reading And Curriculum Positioning

Story Sprout uses curriculum records to connect each story to a reading objective. Examples include:

- Phonics and foundational reading
- Fluency
- Vocabulary
- Comprehension
- Oral language
- Writing response
- Reading confidence and social-emotional learning

Parents see the plain-language objective and the related standard code. Common standard abbreviations include:

- `RF` = Reading Foundational Skills
- `RL` = Reading Literature
- `RI` = Reading Informational Text
- `L` = Language
- `W` = Writing

The curriculum connection is an instructional aid. Story Sprout should not be marketed as a replacement for a school curriculum, teacher evaluation, or professional reading assessment.

## Assessment Experience

New stories use objective multiple-choice questions with two or three answer choices. The questions are designed to be answerable from the story itself.

The child receives:

- The number of correct answers
- A score out of 100
- Encouragement to keep practicing when needed
- A retry option
- A clear way to close the activity

The score measures understanding of that story's questions. It should not be described as a complete measure of reading mastery or a formal educational assessment.

## AI Role

AI is used to generate personalized story content, comprehension questions, and vocabulary support based on the adult's selected learner, grade, reading objective, theme, adventure, and story length.

The application also applies:

- Structured output validation
- Child-safety filtering
- Output moderation
- Grade-level guidance
- Story length limits
- AI usage limits

Adults should still review generated content for suitability and accuracy before using it with a child.

## Privacy And Adult Control

Story Sprout is designed around adult-managed accounts:

- Adults create and manage accounts.
- Learner profiles are private to the signed-in account.
- Children do not create accounts.
- Families can review privacy and data information.
- Adults can export account data.
- Adults can delete account and learner data through the privacy controls.
- Payment details are handled by the payment provider rather than stored by Story Sprout.

Public marketing should accurately describe privacy and legal protections and should not claim compliance certifications unless those have been formally reviewed and confirmed.

## Claims To Avoid Without Further Review

Do not market Story Sprout as:

- A replacement for a teacher
- A formal diagnostic reading assessment
- A guaranteed way to improve reading scores
- A substitute for a school curriculum
- A system that proves reading mastery automatically
- Legally certified COPPA- or FERPA-compliant unless qualified review confirms the claim
- A service where children independently create accounts
- A therapy, medical, psychological, or special-education assessment tool

## Best Customer Promise

Story Sprout makes reading practice more personal by combining a child's imagination with an adult's guidance and a clear reading goal.

## Marketing Summary

Story Sprout is a personalized reading-story platform for families and educators. Adults choose the learner, grade level, reading objective, story length, and adventure. Children help shape the story, read or listen to it, answer objective comprehension questions, and receive immediate story-check feedback. The experience is designed to support reading confidence, vocabulary, comprehension, and meaningful conversation while keeping the adult in control.

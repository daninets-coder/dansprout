# Story quality and family pilot review

Automated checks are structural safeguards, not educator approval. Do not label unreviewed AI output educator-reviewed.

## Sample review
Review examples from PreK, K, grades 1–2, 3–5, and 6–8, including quick and longer stories, varied interests, and parent topic exclusions. Use fictional learners. Record story ID, grade, length, reviewer, date, each score, and specific text supporting the score. Avoid copying child data into review files.

Score each dimension 0 (fails), 1 (needs revision), or 2 (meets):
- Coherence: clear beginning, problem, and resolution; consistent characters.
- Reading fit: sentence complexity and vocabulary fit the requested level.
- Personalization: an interest influences a meaningful plot action.
- Vocabulary: selected words occur in the text and have accurate, accessible definitions.
- Assessment: each question has one defensible answer; record supporting sentence/page; distractors are distinct; no outside knowledge is needed.
- Safety and preferences: age-appropriate content respects every topic exclusion.

Acceptance: at least 10/12, no zero, and full marks for assessment and safety. Revise failed samples and review again. Human review remains required for these semantic criteria.

## Unassisted family pilot
Observe 5–10 consenting adult/child pairs using the app. Do not coach unless they ask for help. Record anonymous observations, not child names.
1. Can the adult add a learner and create the first story?
2. Can they start reading, change text size, play/stop narration, and resume?
3. Can they complete a story and submit questions without confusion?
4. Can the adult explain what the story check does and does not measure?
5. At the next session, can they continue reading and name one useful takeaway?

Record task completion, time, assistance requested, failure point, and optional adult feedback. Invite a return session after one week; report actual returned/invited counts rather than claiming retention from button clicks. Target at least 80% unassisted first-session completion before expanding the pilot. This is a pilot target, not an observed result.

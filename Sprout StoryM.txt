Sprout Story — Marketing Strategy & Growth Foundation 2
01 • STRATEGIC FOUNDATION
The job is not to market an education app. It is to create a product experience parents can understand, children want to repeat, and
families want to share.
NORTH STAR POSITIONING MARKETING JOB
Make reading practice feel personally
meaningful — then prove that the fun is
producing learning.
Learning That Doesn’t Feel Like Work.
Personalized reading as an active alternative to
passive screen time and dry drill-style practice.
Move a parent from “this is cute” to “this is for
my child” to “this works” — with the product
doing the proving.
THE STRATEGIC CHAIN
1. RECOGNITION 2. MAGIC 3. PROOF 4. PRIDE 5. HABIT
“That’s my child.” “That’s my story.” “They actually read it.” “Look what they made.” “Let’s do the next one.”
FOUNDATION PRINCIPLE
Do not scale acquisition before the activation
moment is consistently strong. The no-signup
experience is the bridge between the promise in
the ad and the value of the product.

02 • AUDIENCE & MESSAGE ARCHITECTURE
Build around the parent’s job-to-be-done, while designing every touchpoint so the child becomes the emotional proof.
PRIMARY BUYER CHILD EXPERIENCE TRUST LAYER
Parent / caregiver
Wants reading progress without another battle, while
feeling confident the product is useful and safe.
Hero, not student
The child chooses a world/adventure, sees their name,
reads/listens, answers a short quiz and earns visible
progress.
Safety + transparency
Privacy and safety messaging should be prominent. The
source deck explicitly calls for “COPPA-Compliant &
AI-Guarded” trust language.
MESSAGE LADDER
EMOTIONAL FUNCTIONAL PROOF ACTION
Your child is the hero. Personalized stories matched to reading
goals.
Story + comprehension quiz + progress
feedback.
Create your child’s story.
CREATIVE RULE
Lead with the child’s identity and emotional reaction. Follow with educational proof. Avoid feature dumps.

03 • THE GROWTH FUNNEL
One system, four jobs. Each stage has a different promise and a different success signal.
01 • AWARENESS 02 • ACTIVATION 03 • VIRALITY 04 • RETENTION

Assets: Meta/TikTok creative, seasonal
hooks, diagnostic, teacher starter kits.
Job: deliver the magic before friction
Promise: “See it for yourself.”
Assets: no-signup sandbox, first story, AI

Job: turn pride into distribution
Promise: “Share what they made.”
Assets: achievement links, Story Gift,
milestone covers, co-hero referrals.
Job: create a reason to return
Promise: “There is another story

snapshots, birthday adventures.

OPERATING RULE
Every campaign should be mapped to one funnel
job. If an asset tries to do awareness, conversion,
retention and education all at once, simplify it.

05 • CONVERSION & ACTIVATION
The strongest acquisition system is one where the ad promise and first product experience are almost identical.
AD LAND MAGIC PROOF NEXT
Personalized hero No-signup sandbox Build a story Quiz + progress Sign up / subscribe
FRICTION TO REMOVE
FRICTION DESIGN RESPONSE
Account wall before value Let adults and children build the story, complete the quiz and see progress before sign-up.
Unclear educational value Make comprehension feedback visible and understandable immediately.
Generic creative Personalize the visual hook: child name, companion, world or interest.
Weak reason to return Weekly episodes, progress snapshots and birthday adventures.
Referral feels like marketing Make sharing a child achievement or “Story Gift,” not a generic referral request.
COMMERCIAL PRINCIPLE
Treat the free starter as a proof mechanism, not
just a pricing tier. The source deck’s ladder is 1
story/week with basic text + quiz score, then paid at
$7.99/month for unlimited stories, multi-chapter
series, full AI narration and richer parent reporting.

07 • MEASUREMENT & EXPERIMENTATION
Measure the system in the same order the customer experiences it. Do not let cheap clicks hide weak activation.
STAGE CORE METRIC WHAT IT TELLS US
Awareness Qualified CTR / engaged view rate Does the creative make the right parent curious?
Activation Story-start → story-complete rate Does the product deliver the promised magic?
Learning proof Quiz completion + progress-view rate Does fun connect to visible learning value?
Conversion Starter → paid conversion Does demonstrated value justify payment?
Retention Week 1 / Week 4 return rate Is there a durable habit?
Virality Share rate + referred activation Does pride create organic growth?
Economics CAC, payback, LTV trend Can the acquisition engine scale responsibly?
EXPERIMENTATION SYSTEM
TEST ONE VARIABLE KEEP A WEEKLY LEARNING LOG SCALE ONLY WHAT SURVIVES
Hook, visual, CTA, audience, landing experience or offer
— not five changes at once.
Record hypothesis → test → result → decision. Build
institutional memory.
Winning ads become templates; winning product moments
become product loops.

of high-quality experiments — not a larger list of
tasks.

09 • 90-DAY BUILD ROADMAP
Build the foundation in sequence: prove the message, prove activation, then scale the loops.
PHASE 1 • FOUNDATION PHASE 2 • PROOF PHASE 3 • SCALE
Weeks 1–4
Lock positioning and message hierarchy.
Define core audience hypotheses.
Instrument funnel events.
Build no-signup activation path.
Create Meta/TikTok creative system.
Weeks 5–8
Run structured creative tests.
Measure activation and story completion.
Test free starter → paid path.
Launch achievement sharing.
Start weekly learning rhythm.
Weeks 9–12
Scale winning creative patterns.
Expand seasonal/community programs.
Improve retention loops.
Formalize referral mechanics.
Set CAC/payback guardrails.
NON-NEGOTIABLES
1 Sell the moment of recognition before the feature list.
2 Keep the first experience low-friction and tangible.
3 Show learning proof through story + quiz + progress.
4 Turn finished stories into shareable pride.
5 Protect trust with visible privacy and safety messaging.
6 Scale only after activation and retention are understood.

# Story Sprout 30-Second Video Ad

## Format

- Length: 30 seconds
- Recommended format: vertical 9:16 for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts
- Resolution: 1080 x 1920
- Audience: parents and guardians of young readers
- Tone: warm, trustworthy, imaginative, adult-guided
- Music: gentle piano with light storybook percussion
- Voice: calm, friendly adult narrator

## Core Message

Story Sprout turns a child's interests into a personalized reading adventure while an adult stays in control of the learning experience.

## Scene Plan

### 0-3 seconds: Opening

Visual:

- Story Sprout logo with the four-reader-and-sprout mark.
- Soft movement toward a bright storybook world.

On-screen text:

```text
What if reading practice felt made just for them?
```

Voiceover:

```text
What if reading practice felt made just for them?
```

### 3-8 seconds: Choose the reader

Visual:

- Show the authenticated workspace.
- Show the learner selector and age range.
- Use a gentle zoom toward the learner profile area.

On-screen text:

```text
Start with one important reader.
```

Voiceover:

```text
Start with the reader, their age, and what they love.
```

### 8-14 seconds: Add interests

Visual:

- Show interests such as space, animals, dancing, or science.
- Show the optional topics-to-avoid field.
- Use the existing learner form visual style.

On-screen text:

```text
Their interests. Their comfort. Their story.
```

Voiceover:

```text
Add their interests and any topics your family would like to avoid.
```

### 14-20 seconds: Create the story

Visual:

- Show the story creator.
- Select a reading goal, story world, adventure, and story length.
- Transition into a banner image such as the Moonlight, Ocean, or Matheus Viana story-world image.

On-screen text:

```text
A personalized story with a reading purpose.
```

Voiceover:

```text
Choose a reading goal and an adventure. Story Sprout creates a personalized story with age-appropriate reading practice.
```

### 20-25 seconds: Read and discuss

Visual:

- Show the story reader.
- Show vocabulary and comprehension questions.
- Show the Welcome back activity summary.

On-screen text:

```text
Read. Talk. Practice. Return.
```

Voiceover:

```text
Read together, explore new words, answer story questions, and notice progress over time.
```

### 25-30 seconds: Closing

Visual:

- Return to the Story Sprout logo.
- Show a warm story-world image in the background.
- Keep the next section of the page subtly visible.

On-screen text:

```text
Story Sprout
Personalized reading stories for growing readers.

Start at dansprout.com
```

Voiceover:

```text
Story Sprout. Personalized reading stories for growing readers. Visit dansprout.com.
```

## AI Video Generator Prompt

```text
Create a warm, polished 30-second vertical advertisement for Story Sprout, a parent-managed personalized reading web app for children. The visual language should feel like a modern illustrated storybook: soft natural colors, warm paper whites, leafy greens, coral accents, clear readable typography, and gentle page-turn transitions. Show an adult choosing a learner profile, adding interests such as space, animals, dancing, and science, choosing a grade-level reading objective, selecting a story world, creating a personalized story, reading it together, reviewing vocabulary, and answering simple comprehension questions. Show four diverse young readers represented symbolically around a small growing sprout in the logo. Keep the adult visibly in control. Avoid portraying children creating independent accounts. Avoid claims of guaranteed reading improvement, formal diagnosis, or automatic mastery. End with the Story Sprout logo and the text: “Personalized reading stories for growing readers. Start at dansprout.com.” Use calm, hopeful music and a friendly adult narrator.
```

## Claims To Avoid In The Video

Do not say:

- Guaranteed reading improvement
- Mastery automatically measured
- Formal reading assessment
- Replacement for a teacher
- COPPA certified
- FERPA certified
- Every story is perfectly safe

Use:

- Supports reading practice
- Story-specific feedback
- Adult-guided experience
- Age-appropriate guidance
- Review each generated story
- Curriculum-informed reading objective
